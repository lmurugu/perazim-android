# Creed and confession texts: source record

## Apostles' Creed (received form)
- Text: English received form, "I. The Apostles' Creed (a) Received Form", as printed in Philip Schaff, *Creeds of Christendom*, Vol. II (1877). Public domain.
- Retrieved 2026-11-24 from the CCEL plain-text cache, https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2/cache/creeds2.txt. Bracketed variants ("[flesh]", "[Hades, spirit-world]") are kept as printed.
- Licence: public domain (CCEL rights line to be confirmed before release).
- File: apostles-creed.txt. Status: DRAFT_PENDING_LEVI.

## Westminster Confession of Faith (1646), English text
- Text: "The Confession of Faith of the Assembly of Divines at Westminster", the 1946 transcription edited by S.W. Carruthers, from the 1646 manuscript. Retrieved 2026-11-24 from https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Confession_of_Faith_of_the_Assembly_of_Divines_at_Westminster.
- Wikisource says the work is public domain worldwide because the author died at least 100 years ago. The Wikisource transcription itself is under CC BY-SA 4.0 (page terms). Levi or the licence check must confirm this before release.
- The Wikisource page offers the 1647 "Humble Advice" text too, but its chapter pages are incomplete and carry long-s artifacts, so it was not used.
- The body text was taken from the page. Footnote numbers and page templates were removed. Chapter headings were normalised (CHAPTER XXI. had no period). The signatures of the Assembly were omitted. Curly apostrophes were changed to straight ones. No words were changed.
- File: westminster-confession-1647.txt (33 chapters). Status: DRAFT_PENDING_LEVI. Historical confession, not a Perazim position.
