## 1. Purpose

Disputed practical and theological subjects are handled in **separate** position papers, each following the same nine-part template. They are not combined, because each involves different texts and pastoral consequences.

## 3. The nine-part template

Each position paper contains:

1. Biblical passages and their original contexts.
2. Relevant Hebrew or Greek terms, where they materially affect interpretation.
3. The wider biblical teaching.
4. Historical Christian interpretations.
5. Competing evangelical positions.
6. Perazim's proposed position (**pending** unless approved).
7. Pastoral implications and exceptions.
8. What the AI may and may not say.
9. Authorising approval and version.

## 4. Status model

Each paper carries one status from G01 section 5: Approved, Established biblical teaching, Under review, Unresolved, or Superseded.

All five papers start as **Under review** or **Unresolved**. None is approved.

## 5. Relationship to other documents

- P5 complements G01's "Unresolved matters" so that women in ministry is not left permanently open without a decision.
- Position papers do not override Scripture. Their status determines only what the AI may say as Perazim's view.
