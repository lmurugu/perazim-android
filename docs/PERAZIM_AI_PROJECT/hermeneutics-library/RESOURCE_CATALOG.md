## Important exclusions and cautions

- ESV: excluded from AI processing under Crossway's current published terms unless written permission expressly authorises it.
- Sefaria, ORACC, Perseus, CDLI and other aggregators: item-, project- or component-specific licences; do not bulk-import based on a parent repository's label.
- CC BY-NC and CC BY-SA: keep quarantined pending licence review for the intended church distribution.
- Historic scans: public-domain status of the original work does not automatically clear every modern edition, scan, transcription, photograph or database compilation.
- The KJV and Swahili Union Version rights statements in the existing project document must be rechecked for the exact assets, editions and jurisdictions.
- Proprietary reference works such as BDAG, HALOT, major modern critical editions and copyrighted apparatus are not approved for copying merely because they are academically valuable.
