# Perazim app voice guide (English)

Status: Draft. Owner-approved. Version 0.1. Last reviewed: 2026-11-18.

## Purpose
The app should sound like a person who knows the member and cares how they are doing. It should not sound like a task list or a company.

## Principles
1. **Speak to the person, not the task.** Use the member's first name where it feels natural. Respond to what they have just done or are carrying.
2. **Invite, don't instruct.** Prefer "Would you like to bring this to God today?" over "Go and pray."
3. **Let them lead.** Offer a next step without requiring it. Make "not today" easy to choose.
4. **Vary the wording.** Repeated phrasing makes the app feel automated. Keep several versions of each message.
5. **Know when to be quiet.** After a hard week, or after a prayer request, a short message of support is enough. No call to action.
6. **Be honest and modest.** Celebrate faithfulness, not performance. Never imply that God's favour depends on streaks.
7. **Never guilt.** No "you'll lose your streak", "you missed", or "don't break the chain".
8. **Keep the scripture central.** Quote or name the passage. Don't paraphrase it into a slogan.

## Rules for notifications
- Notifications appear on the lock screen, so they must be generic and must never mention a topic, a prayer, a person's name or a health matter.
- Use general wording: "Your reading is ready when you have a quiet moment."
- The warm, specific wording appears only once the app is open.

## Examples

| Moment | Message |
|---|---|
| Finished a reading | "Thank you for sitting with Psalm 23 this morning, Grace. If anything stayed with you, you can bring it to God now." |
| Milestone | "Five mornings in a row. That's faithfulness, and it shows." |
| Unread messages | "Peter and two others have written to you." |
| After a hard week | "I'm sorry this week has been heavy. You don't have to be okay today. We're praying with you." |
| After a prayer request is posted | "Thank you for trusting us with this. We're holding it with you." |
| Pointing to a pastor | "If you'd like to talk more, or need support, you can speak with your pastor. Thank you for sharing with us." |
| Friend activity | "Grace prayed for you this morning." (shown only inside the app, and only to friends) |
| Nothing new today | "Nothing new yet today. Your passage will be here whenever you're ready." |
| Error | "We couldn't reach the server just now. Your prayers are saved on your phone and will send when you're back online." |

## Things to avoid
- Commands: "Read now", "Complete your task", "Log your prayer".
- Counts used as pressure: "You're 2 days away from a badge".
- Exclamation marks in serious moments.
- Slang, jokes about scripture, and urgency.
- Generic praise: "Great job!"

## Review
The app owner approves wording. Feedback from pilot members will update this guide. A Swahili version is planned for a later release.
