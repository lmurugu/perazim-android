## 2. What this policy covers
This policy covers the Perazim Android app and the Perazim server. It does not cover third-party websites or services that the app links to, such as the M-Pesa app, WhatsApp or Google Maps. Those apply their own policies.

## 3. Information we collect
- **Account:** full name, telephone number, campus, role, a PIN (stored only as a salted hash), a random account identifier, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
- **Profile:** optional biography and avatar image.
- **Community:** posts, comments, prayer requests, testimonies, group and event activity, RSVPs, friend connections, private messages, and photos you upload.
- **Friend activity:** if you have chosen to show when you were last active, your friends can see "active today" or "active this week". You can switch this off at any time.
- **Membership:** if you submit a membership claim, the claim and its review result are stored separately from your account. A claim does not make you a confirmed member.
- **Devotional progress:** reading and prayer activity, streak, points, and challenge completions.
- **Learning preferences (optional, see section 5):** topics you choose to grow in, and topic tags the app records when you finish readings or prayers. The app does not store the words you typed in a search or question.
- **Security and service records:** sign-in events, session tokens, failed sign-in counts, and request logs needed to keep the service secure.
- **Privacy records:** your acknowledgements of this policy and its versions, your consent choices with their dates, and your data-request history.

We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs or crash-reporting SDKs in the current release. If that changes, this policy and the in-app consent controls will be updated first.

## 4. Religious and other sensitive information
Church membership, prayer requests, testimonies, Bible searches and questions about faith, illness, family or other personal matters can reveal religious belief or health. Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, belief and health are sensitive personal data. We handle this information with extra care:
- we process it only for the purpose you chose, such as answering your question or showing a community post to the people you chose;
- we do not use it to send notifications. Notifications are always generic, for example "Your reading is ready", and never mention a topic, a prayer request or a health matter;
- we do not keep the words you typed in a search or question. We keep only a topic tag, and only if you have turned on personalisation;
- you can see and delete what has been stored under Settings → Privacy & data.

If you use the app to ask about another person's illness or circumstances, please share only what you need to get the answer. Do not enter another person's full medical or personal details. We do not keep a record of a named third party's details.

## 5. How we use information
Each purpose below has its own choice. Nothing is switched on for you by default unless the table says so.

| Purpose | What is used | Your choice |
|---|---|---|
| Running your account and showing community content to the audiences you choose | Account, profile, community content | Required to use the account |
| Answering a question or search you make | The question text, for the answer only | Used when you ask; not kept after the answer is shown |
| Personalised suggestions (readings, prayers and topics related to what you have chosen or finished) | Topics you chose, and topic tags from readings and prayers you finished | Off by default. Turn on or off at any time |
| Notifications | Your notification choices. The text is always generic | Off by default. Turn on or off at any time |
| Friend activity and "active today" | Last-active time, shared only with friends | Off by default. Turn on or off at any time |
| Keeping the service secure | Sign-in and request records | Required |
| Meeting legal obligations | Records you are required to keep | Required by law |

Choosing "no" to any optional purpose does not limit the rest of the app.

## 6. Who can see your information
- **Other members** can see your name, avatar, biography and content you post to a group, community feed or event, according to that feature's audience. Friends can also see your activity status, if you have turned it on.
- **Friends** see the community content you share with them, and nothing else.
- **Church leaders and administrators** can see information that their role requires, such as membership records, claims, moderation queues and attendance or activity relevant to their pastoral or administrative duties. Which roles can see what is set by the church and is listed in the in-app notice. Every view by a leader or administrator is recorded in an audit log.
- **Service providers** (section 8) process data only to provide the service.

## 7. Online AI
When you use the optional online AI, your question and limited conversation context are sent to our relay, which calls Google Gemini. The question you type is part of what is sent. It is not sent with your name or phone number. Do not enter other people's personal information. The online AI is off in release builds unless the owner enables it. ## 10. Your rights
You can, from Settings → Privacy & data:
- request a copy of your data;
- ask us to correct your data;
- withdraw any optional consent, including personalisation, notifications and friend activity;
- delete your account.
You can also make a request through the public web form linked from the app. We respond within the time set by law. You may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya.

## 11. Children
You must be 18 or older to create an account. Under Article 260 of the Constitution of Kenya, a child is a person under 18. We do not create accounts for children, so we do not collect children's personal data through the app. If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 18, we close it and delete its personal data. A parent or guardian who believes a child has given us information can contact us, and we will delete it. Reading the Bible, hymns and sermons does not require an account and does not involve personal data.

## 12. Security
We use encryption in transit, hashed credentials, role-based access checks on the server, and an encrypted session-token store on the device. No system is perfectly secure. Report a suspected security problem through the process in `SECURITY.md`.

## 13. Changes
We will show the new version in the app and ask you to acknowledge it when it changes materially. Each version you acknowledge is recorded with the date.
