# Community Guidelines

Status: Draft (not approved). Version 0.1. Last reviewed: 2026-11-18.

Perazim is a church community. These guidelines apply to posts, comments, prayer requests, testimonies, group and event activity, photos and private messages.

## What is not allowed
- Harassment, bullying, threats, or hate speech against any person or group.
- Sexual content, or content that sexualises children in any form. Such content is reported to the authorities.
- Sharing other people's personal information (phone numbers, addresses, health details) without their consent.
- Fraud, scams, or requests for money or personal details under false pretences.
- Impersonating a church leader or member.
- Spam, unsolicited marketing, or misleading claims about healing or finances.
- Content that is violent, or that promotes illegal activity.

## Prayer and testimonies
Share prayer requests and testimonies only with the audience you intend. Anonymous posts are still visible to the service and to moderators.

## Children
Only adults aged 18 or over may hold an account, post, comment or send messages. Accounts that belong to a person under 18 are closed. If you see content that puts a child at risk, report it at once. We will remove it and report it to the authorities as the law requires.

## Reporting and blocking
Use **Report** on any post, comment or message, and **Block** on any user. Reports are reviewed by church moderators. We aim to act on reports within 24 hours and to tell you the outcome.

## Consequences
Depending on the breach we may remove content, hide it, restrict posting, suspend or remove an account. Serious or unlawful content is reported to the authorities.

## Appeals
If you believe a decision was wrong, contact the church through the contact in the Privacy Policy. A different moderator reviews the appeal.
