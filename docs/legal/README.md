These are drafts for review. They are not legal advice and must be approved by the owner (and counsel, if engaged) before publication. Each document's status line must read `Status: Approved` only after approval, and `scripts/check_legal_docs.sh` enforces that approved documents contain no placeholders.

Placeholders are written as `[PENDING OWNER INPUT: …]`. Publication also requires gate L4 (privacy notice finalized, ODPC position decided) and the Play Data Safety form, which must match these documents.

The minimum age of 18 is approved by the owner (2026-11-18). The legal text on age is final for that rule; the other documents still need approval.
