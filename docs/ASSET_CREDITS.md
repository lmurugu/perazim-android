# Game scene credits

Each game scene is bundled in the APK (offline) as compressed webp under `src/main/assets/games/scenes/`. Scenes below are public-domain or CC0 images from Openverse, chosen for their theme. No people or copyrighted characters.

The vineyard, lamp-lit room and story road scenes (`vine_scene`, `lamp_scene`, `story_scene`) are original art generated for this project and approved by Levi.
