## Mandatory completion before publication or operational use
- Confirm PMCI's exact registered legal identity and governing authority.
- Appoint the responsible privacy contact and fill all placeholders.
- Confirm ODPC registration status and complete any required registration.
- Document lawful basis by purpose; do not assume blanket consent or automatic application of section 45(a).
- Complete the DPIA using actual infrastructure, provider locations and data flows.
- Confirm all processors, subprocessors, hosting/backup countries and written agreements.
- Approve retention periods and rights-request procedures.
- Confirm the real 65-record CSV has not entered test databases, logs, APKs, repository history or agent workspaces; investigate any exposure.
- Keep the register server-side only. Remove contact auto-verification. Human administrator approval is mandatory.
- Have a qualified Kenyan data-protection professional review the final pack.
