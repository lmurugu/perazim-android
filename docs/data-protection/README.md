# Perazim data protection working package

This folder preserves the supplied PMCI data-protection pack v0.1. The Word originals remain editable and unchanged in `working-drafts/originals/`. Their supplied status is **DRAFT FOR REVIEW**; this repository does not treat them as institutionally or legally approved.

The public policy and Perazim app notice have text renditions in `public/`; the current renditions are factual review drafts, not approved or published documents. The corrected app notice is version 0.2 and is copied to the Android privacy asset for review, but it is not the backend's active notice and is not presented as published. The server remains fail-closed while its configured notice is a draft. The internal access policy, DPIA working assessment, retention schedule, breach response, technical requirements, vendor checklist, and implementation guide remain in the controlled working-drafts folder and are not public API content.

The policy and notice now describe the current PIN flow (no OTP provider), searchable authenticated directory fields, AI transmission, request-intake limits, unverified at-rest encryption and backups, and the absence of age/guardian controls. Kenyan statutory section references and all legal interpretations remain **UNVERIFIED — counsel review required**. Do not treat this text, the Android copy, or a passing software test as legal or institutional approval.

Before publication, PMCI must resolve the placeholders and approvals identified in the source documents, including its exact legal identity and contacts, lawful bases, sensitive-data conditions, children’s safeguards, ODPC registration position, retention periods, hosting/processor details, cross-border transfers, and notice effective date. The DPIA remains a working assessment.

## Official reference check

The [ODPC regulatory framework page](https://www.odpc.go.ke/data-protection-laws-kenya/index.html) links the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the 2021 General and Registration Regulations. The ODPC FAQ currently lists faith-based or religious institutions among categories not exempt from mandatory registration. PMCI must confirm its own registration position with the ODPC or Kenyan counsel; this package does not make that determination. See the [Act](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf), [General Regulations](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf), [Registration Regulations](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-REGISTRATION-OF-DATA-CONTROLLERS-AND-DATA-PROCESSORS-REGULATIONS-2021.pdf), and [ODPC registration FAQ](https://www.odpc.go.ke/faqs/).
