# Privacy notice and policy institutional review checklist

**Status:** Internal review aid. The policy and notice remain drafts and are not published or active for registration.

Resolve and document these items before approving or publishing a notice version:

1. **Controller identity and responsibility**
   - Confirm the legal name and address of the responsible PMCI entity.
   - Name the accountable privacy lead and provide a monitored privacy contact channel.
   - Confirm the process for receiving requests from data subjects and their representatives.

2. **Purpose and lawful-basis decisions**
   - Approve a purpose-by-purpose inventory for registration, membership claims and verification, account security, community features, prayer/testimony content, analytics and any AI feature.
   - Select and document the lawful basis for each purpose, including any special-category or religious-belief data and any optional processing.
   - Decide whether any optional purpose requires a separate, withdrawable consent control. Do not describe the notice acknowledgement as consent.

3. **Recipients and service providers**
   - Identify each hosting, database, messaging, analytics, backup, AI and support provider that actually receives data.
   - Record the data each provider receives, its purpose, location, access controls, retention and applicable transfer arrangements.
   - Confirm whether an AI provider is used. Current Android online AI is disabled pending a verified authenticated server contract; do not describe the prior relay as an approved active processor.

4. **Data inventory and access**
   - Confirm the current Android and backend fields, collection points, roles and access paths.
   - Confirm which community content can be public, private or administrator-only, including prayer requests, testimonies, messages and uploaded images.
   - Confirm age eligibility and whether an age-check or guardian process is required. The app currently has no age check or guardian flow.

5. **Retention, backups and deletion**
   - Approve a category-specific retention schedule with event triggers and accountable owners.
   - Define backup expiry, restore handling and deletion propagation; document the actual cycle before making claims about backup expiration.
   - Define preservation exceptions and the audit evidence retained after a request.

6. **Rights request operations**
   - Approve identity verification, routing, response ownership and deadlines for access, correction and erasure requests.
   - Decide and implement the operational processes for objection, restriction and portability before claiming they can be exercised through the app.
   - Confirm how requests involving membership or pastoral records are reviewed and safeguarded.

7. **Security and incident response**
   - Verify encryption at rest for each database, file store, backup and provider; distinguish verified controls from planned controls.
   - Review transport security, administrator access, logging, incident escalation and breach communications.
   - Confirm whether any credential previously packaged in an APK remains active and have the credential owner rotate or revoke it. No revocation is asserted by this checklist.

8. **Document control and acknowledgement**
   - Counsel/institutional reviewers must verify statutory references, legal descriptions and any required wording.
   - Approve a final notice and policy version, content hash, effective date, public URL and publication authority.
   - Ensure Android acknowledgement wording, server-stored wording, version and hash match the approved record before registration is enabled.
   - Keep registration fail-closed while the required notice is draft, absent or withdrawn.

Record each decision, reviewer, date and supporting evidence in the institutional document-control process. Do not replace unresolved items with assumed provider names, legal conclusions, contacts, retention periods or approval dates.
