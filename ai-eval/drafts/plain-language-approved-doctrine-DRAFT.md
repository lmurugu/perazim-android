## 1. Baptism in the Holy Spirit and tongues

**What we believe.** Being baptised in the Holy Spirit is a work of God that is separate from becoming a Christian. Speaking in tongues is normal and good, and we welcome it. But tongues are not the only proof that someone has received the Spirit's baptism.

**Scripture.** Acts 2:4; Acts 8:17; Acts 19:2; Romans 8:9; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 12:30.

**Other views, briefly.** Some teach that Spirit baptism is a second experience after conversion, and that tongues always prove it. Others teach that the sign gifts ended with the apostles. [to decide: one line judging each from Scripture]

**How to apply it.** Ask God to fill you with His Spirit. Test every experience by the Word of God.

## 2. Prosperity teaching, giving and seed offerings

**What we believe.** God calls us to give generously and gladly. God's blessing cannot be bought with a "seed" or a payment. Pressure and manipulation in giving are wrong. Tithing is not settled, and we do not teach it as a position.

**Scripture.** 2 Corinthians 9:7; Acts 20:35; Acts 8:20; Proverbs 22:16; 1 Timothy 6:17.

**Other views, briefly.** Prosperity teaching says faith and giving bring money back as a rule. Scripture warns against treating God's gifts as a deal (Acts 8:20). Some give as worship and trust, with no promised return. That fits Scripture. [to decide: confirm the evaluation of prosperity teaching]

**How to apply it.** Give from a willing heart. Never give because you were pressed, or because a leader promised a return.

## 3. Polygamy and families already in polygamous marriages

**What we believe.** God's design is one man and one woman in marriage (Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:8). Church leaders are to be the husband of one wife (1 Timothy 3:2; Titus 1:6). Families already in a polygamous marriage receive pastoral care. They are not told to break up or to abandon wives and children.

**Other views, briefly.** Some read the Old Testament polygamy as recorded, not approved. Others apply the leader rule more widely. [to decide: one line judging each from Scripture]

**How to apply it.** Pastors walk with these families patiently. Every new marriage is between one man and one woman.

## 4. Dowry and bride wealth

**What we believe.** Dowry is an acceptable custom when it honours both families. It must never become selling a person, a debt trap, or a reason to delay marriage.

**Scripture.** Genesis 2:24; Exodus 22:16; Proverbs 18:22; 1 Peter 3:7.

**Other views, briefly.** Some see dowry as a covenant honouring both families. Others see the risk that it makes a person into a price, creates debt, or delays marriage. Our view keeps the first and guards against the second. [to decide: confirm]

**How to apply it.** Let the families honour each other. No one is sold, and no one is kept waiting.

## 5. The Trinity and the person of Christ (established orthodoxy)

**What we believe.** There is one God in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. This is the teaching of the Nicene and Chalcedonian statements, which are established orthodoxy.

**Scripture.** John 1:1; John 1:14; Matthew 28:19; Colossians 2:9.

**How to apply it.** Worship Jesus as God, and follow Him as your Saviour and Lord.

## 6. Penal substitution in the atonement

**What we believe.** Jesus took the punishment our sins deserved, in our place. This is a major Reformation teaching about the cross. It is not the only way the Bible describes what the cross achieved.

**Scripture.** Isaiah 53:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 2:24.

**Other views, briefly.** Some describe the cross mainly as a victory over evil, or as a display of God's love. These point to real truths of Scripture. Our view keeps penal substitution at the centre. [to decide: confirm]

**How to apply it.** Thank God for what Jesus did in your place, and live in that gratitude.
