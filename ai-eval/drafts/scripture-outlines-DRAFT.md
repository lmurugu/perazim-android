# Scripture-only outlines: DRAFT for Levi's review (NOT APPROVED, kept on decline)

Status: DRAFT. These four outlines stay on their fixed decline until Levi approves them. Each one uses two or more Bible passages, read in context. Verses are referenced here and quoted word for word from the King James Version in the app.

Labels: "Scripture says" marks what the text states directly. "Working interpretation, under review" marks how we read several passages together.

---

## 1. Apostasy and assurance (working interpretation, under review)

**Scripture says directly.**
- Those who are truly Christ's keep their faith and continue to the end (Hebrews 3:14; Matthew 24:13).
- Nothing can separate believers from the love of God in Christ (Romans 8:38–39).
- Warnings in Hebrews are real and serious, and they call us to hold fast (Hebrews 6:4–6; 10:26–27, 35–39).

**Working interpretation, under review.** We understand that a person who has truly come to Christ is kept by God, and will not finally fall away (John 10:27–29; Philippians 1:6). We take the warning passages as real and serious, and as the means God uses to keep His people. We do not judge any individual's destiny (John 10:28; 1 Corinthians 4:5).

**Application.** Keep walking in faith, confess sin, and hold fast to Christ in the community of believers.

## 2. Election and the preaching of the gospel (working interpretation, under review)

**Scripture says directly.**
- God chose His people in Christ before the foundation of the world (Ephesians 1:4–5).
- The gospel is to be preached to every creature (Mark 16:15; Matthew 28:19).
- Whoever believes will be saved, and Christ calls all to come (John 3:16; Romans 10:13–14).

**Working interpretation, under review.** We understand that God's choosing is real, and that it is the ground of our assurance, not a reason to stop preaching. God uses the preaching of the gospel as the means by which He calls His chosen people (Romans 10:14–17; Acts 13:48).

**Application.** Share the good news with everyone, and trust God's choosing without guessing who is chosen.

## 3. Grace and obedience (Scripture says directly)

**Scripture says directly.**
- We are saved by grace, through faith, and not by our own works (Ephesians 2:8–9).
- We are created in Christ for good works, which God prepared beforehand (Ephesians 2:10).
- Grace teaches us to deny ungodliness and live soberly and righteously (Titus 2:11–12).
- Faith without works is dead (James 2:17, 26).

**What this means.** Grace is the reason we can obey, and obedience is the fruit of grace, not the payment for it.

**Application.** Obey God out of gratitude, not to earn His love.

## 4. Sovereignty and responsibility (Scripture says directly)

**Scripture says directly.**
- God is sovereign over all things, and His purposes stand (Isaiah 46:9–10; Proverbs 16:9).
- Every person is responsible before God for their own sin (Ezekiel 18:20; Romans 2:6).
- Work out your salvation with fear and trembling, because God is working in you (Philippians 2:12–13).

**What this means.** God's sovereignty and human responsibility are both taught in Scripture. We do not try to resolve every question about how they fit together, and we do not use God's sovereignty to excuse sin.

**Application.** Take responsibility for your choices before God, and trust Him with what you cannot understand.
