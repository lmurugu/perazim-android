## A. Holy Spirit gaps

### A1. Can Christians be possessed by demons?
**Status:** Undecided. Whether a believer can be "possessed" is Levi's open question. The outline below states only what Scripture says about the believer's position and the believer's duty.

Plain-words summary: Scripture says the devil is a real enemy who attacks believers, and it tells us how to stand against him. It also says that those who belong to God are kept by Him and are stronger in Christ than the enemy. It does not give a single word that settles the question of "possession" in the modern sense.

- **James 4:7** (KJV): “Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.”
- **1 Peter 5:8–9** (KJV): “Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour: Whom resist stedfast in the faith, knowing that the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world.”
- **1 John 4:4** (KJV): “Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.”
- **John 10:28** (KJV): “And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand.”

Decision needed from Levi: does the guide say "a believer cannot be possessed", "a believer can be oppressed but not possessed", or "undecided"? Recommended: "undecided", with the verses above.

### A2. Are prophets still operating in the church today?
**Status:** Approved for the gifts continuing (Holy Spirit entry, Levi, 2026-11-24). Prophecy must be tested by Scripture: Approved (spiritual-claims route). The question of which specific people are prophets is not answered here.

Plain-words summary: the gifts continue, and prophecy is a gift to build up the church. Every word that claims to come from God is tested by the Bible, and no word of prophecy overrules Scripture.

- **1 Corinthians 14:1** (KJV): “Follow after charity, and desire spiritual gifts, but rather that ye may prophesy.”
- **1 Corinthians 14:3** (KJV): “But he that prophesieth speaketh unto men to edification, and exhortation, and comfort.”
- **1 Thessalonians 5:19–21** (KJV): “Quench not the Spirit. Despise not prophesyings. Prove all things; hold fast that which is good.”
- **1 Corinthians 14:29** (KJV): “Let the prophets speak two or three, and let the other judge.”

### A3. Can a Christian be healed if they have enough faith?
**Status:** Draft for review. Prayer for the sick and God's power to heal are taught in James 5. Whether healing is included in the atonement is **Undecided** (Levi's list), so the outline does not say it. The idea that faith is a currency that guarantees healing is not taught in Scripture.

Plain-words summary: God heals, and we are told to pray for the sick in faith. But Scripture never treats faith as a price that buys healing, and Paul's own prayer was not answered in the way he asked. We trust God's wise answer.

- **James 5:14–15** (KJV): “Is any sick among you? let him call for the elders of the church; and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord: And the prayer of faith shall save the sick, and the Lord shall raise him up; and if he have committed sins, they shall be forgiven him.”
- **2 Corinthians 12:7–9** (KJV): “And lest I should be exalted above measure through the abundance of the revelations, there was given to me a thorn in the flesh, the messenger of Satan to buffet me, lest I should be exalted above measure. For this thing I besought the Lord thrice, that it might depart from me. And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.”
- **1 Peter 2:24** (KJV): “Who his own self bare our sins in his own body on the tree, that we, being dead to sins, should live unto righteousness: by whose stripes ye were healed.”

Note: 1 Peter 2:24 is cited because it is the passage people use for "healing in the atonement". The outline quotes it only; it does not draw the conclusion, which is Undecided.

## B. Reasoning fallbacks (12 cases, three arguments)

### B1. "Christians disagree about doctrine, so Christianity is false."
**Status:** Working interpretation (under review) for the reasoning. The list of essential doctrines is **Undecided** and is Levi's call.

Plain-words summary: disagreement among people does not prove the message is false. Scripture warns that false teaching would arise among those who claim Jesus, and it tells us to test teachings by the Bible and to hold to the one faith.

- **1 Corinthians 11:19** (KJV): “For there must be also heresies among you, that they which are approved may be made manifest among you.”
- **Acts 20:29–30** (KJV): “For I know this, that after my departing shall grievous wolves enter in among you, not sparing the flock. Also of your own selves shall men arise, speaking perverse things, to draw away disciples after them.”
- **1 John 4:1** (KJV): “Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world.”
- **Ephesians 4:3–6** (KJV): “Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace. There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling; One Lord, one faith, one baptism, One God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all.”

### B2. "If God is all-powerful and all-loving, why does evil exist?"
**Status:** Working interpretation (under review). The explanation that sin entered through the Fall is a standard Reformation and Evangelical answer, but Levi has not signed the wording.

Plain-words summary: God is not the author of sin or temptation. Sin entered through the choice of the first humans, and creation is under the curse. God is able to bring good out of evil, as He did with Joseph, and the Bible does not explain every secret reason.

- **James 1:13** (KJV): “Let no man say when he is tempted, I am tempted of God: for God cannot be tempted with evil, neither tempteth he any man:”
- **Genesis 3:17–19** (KJV): “And unto Adam he said, Because thou hast hearkened unto the voice of thy wife, and hast eaten of the tree, of which I commanded thee, saying, Thou shalt not eat of it: cursed is the ground for thy sake; in sorrow shalt thou eat of it all the days of thy life; Thorns also and thistles shall it bring forth to thee; and thou shalt eat the herb of the field; In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread, till thou return unto the ground; for out of it wast thou taken: for dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return.”
- **Romans 8:20–22** (KJV): “For the creature was made subject to vanity, not willingly, but by reason of him who hath subjected the same in hope, Because the creature itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the glorious liberty of the children of God. For we know that the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now.”
- **Genesis 50:20** (KJV): “But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save much people alive.”
- **Deuteronomy 29:29** (KJV): “The secret things belong unto the LORD our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law.”
- **Romans 8:28** (KJV): “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.”

### B3. "Is the claim that the Bible contradicts itself logically sustainable?"
**Status:** Undecided for the inerrancy wording (Levi's list). This outline states only what Scripture says about itself and gives a method for apparent contradictions. It does not declare the Bible free of error in a formal sense.

Plain-words summary: the Bible claims to be God's word, true and sufficient. Apparent tensions in Scripture are handled by reading the context, the kind of writing, and the question each passage answers. Proverbs gives an example that looks like a contradiction but is answered by wisdom about when to speak.

- **2 Timothy 3:16** (KJV): “All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness:”
- **John 17:17** (KJV): “Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth.”
- **Proverbs 26:4–5** (KJV): “Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest thou also be like unto him. Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own conceit.”
