## 1. Did Jesus rise from the dead? (resurrection evidence)
**Scripture says directly.**
- Christ died for our sins and rose again the third day according to the Scriptures (1 Corinthians 15:3–4).
- He appeared to many witnesses, including more than five hundred at once (1 Corinthians 15:5–8).
- He showed Himself alive with many infallible proofs over forty days (Acts 1:3).
- Thomas saw and touched the risen Lord, and Jesus blessed those who believe without seeing (John 20:24–29).

**Working interpretation, under review.** If Christ is not risen, the apostles' preaching and our faith are empty (1 Corinthians 15:14–17). We read the witness accounts as the strongest evidence, and faith is a response to that evidence, not a leap without it.

**Application.** Read the resurrection accounts for yourself, and share them with a friend.

## 2. Can we trust the Bible we have? (reliability of Scripture)
**Scripture says directly.**
- All Scripture is given by inspiration of God (2 Timothy 3:16).
- Holy men of God spoke as they were moved by the Holy Ghost (2 Peter 1:20–21).
- Luke writes an orderly account, having searched out the facts from eyewitnesses (Luke 1:1–4).
- The Holy Spirit teaches believers all things and brings Christ's words to mind (John 14:26).

**Working interpretation, under review.** Manuscript copies and translations are studied with care, and the differences between copies are real but do not change the core teaching of the faith. [source needed: manuscript evidence, to be checked before use]

**Application.** Read the Bible regularly, compare translations, and ask a pastor when a question remains.

## 3. Did the early church believe Jesus is God, and the Trinity before Nicaea?
**Scripture says directly.**
- The Word was God, and the Word was made flesh (John 1:1, 14).
- Baptism is in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost (Matthew 28:19).
- Ananias lied to the Holy Ghost, and was lying to God (Acts 5:3–4).

**Working interpretation, under review.** The Nicene Creed (325) and the Chalcedonian statement (451) state, in their own words, what the apostolic writings teach. They are supporting witnesses, not authorities above Scripture. [source needed: quotations from early writers, to be checked before use]

**Application.** Worship Jesus as God, because Scripture teaches it.

## 4. Does God exist? (a short argument a skeptic can follow)
**Scripture says directly.**
- The invisible things of God are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made (Romans 1:19–20).
- The heavens declare the glory of God (Psalm 19:1).
- Without faith it is impossible to please God, for whoever comes to God must believe that He is (Hebrews 11:6).

**Working interpretation, under review.** Creation points to a Creator, and the moral sense of people points to a God who judges (Romans 2:14–15). This is a reasoned case from Scripture, not a proof that compels every mind.

**Application.** Look at creation with thankfulness, and ask God to reveal Himself to you.

## 5. Why does evil exist if God is all-powerful and all-loving?
**Scripture says directly.**
- God is not tempted with evil, and He tempts no one (James 1:13).
- All things work together for good to them that love God (Romans 8:28).
- What men meant for evil, God meant for good (Genesis 50:20).

**Working interpretation, under review.** Evil entered through human rebellion (Genesis 3). God's purposes are greater than our understanding, and He will judge and remove evil in the end. We do not claim to explain every detail.

**Application.** Bring suffering honestly to God, and trust His goodness.

## 6. Do Christians disagree, so Christianity is false?
**Scripture says directly.**
- There is one body and one Spirit, one Lord, one faith, one baptism (Ephesians 4:3–6).
- Paul urges believers to speak the same thing and be perfectly joined together (1 Corinthians 1:10).
- The Bereans searched the Scriptures daily to see whether these things were so (Acts 17:11).

**Working interpretation, under review.** Disagreement among believers is real, and it is a reason to return to Scripture, not a reason to reject it. Not every disagreement is about what is essential to the faith.

**Application.** Hold to the essentials, be patient with one another, and test every teaching by the Bible.

## 7. Does the Bible contradict itself?
**Scripture says directly.**
- The sum of Thy word is truth (Psalm 119:160).
- Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth (John 17:17).

**Working interpretation, under review.** Apparent contradictions are read in their context, and many are resolved by careful reading. We do not claim every hard passage is already explained.

**Application.** Read passages in their context, and ask for help with the hard ones.

## 8. Is faith believing without evidence?
**Scripture says directly.**
- Faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen (Hebrews 11:1).
- Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God (Romans 10:17).
- God has given assurance to all men, by raising Christ from the dead (Acts 17:31).

**Working interpretation, under review.** Biblical faith is trust in a God who has shown Himself, not blind belief. It rests on the word and works of God.

**Application.** Trust God because of what He has said and done.

## 9. Who was Augustine, and is he still important?
**Scripture says directly.** Scripture gives no biography of later teachers. Test every teacher's writing by the Bible (Acts 17:11).

**Working interpretation, under review.** Early and later writers can help us understand the faith, but their teaching is checked against Scripture, and it is not an authority above it. [source needed: Augustine's teaching, to be checked before use]
