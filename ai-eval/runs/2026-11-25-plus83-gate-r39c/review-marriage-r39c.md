## MG-S01-HUS
- Set: mirror | Category: submission | Route: routed:approved-obey-in-marriage
- Prompt: My wife says I must obey her even when I believe she is wrong. Is that what the Bible teaches?
- Answer:

  > Scripture does not teach that either spouse must obey the other in everything, and no one must obey a sinful instruction (Acts 5:29). Wives are called to submit to their own husbands in the Lord, and husbands and wives are both called to mutual submission in reverence for Christ (Ephesians 5:21). A husband is called to love his wife as Christ loved the church (Ephesians 5:25), not to demand obedience in every matter. Asking questions and disagreeing honestly is not rebellion. A husband or wife who disagrees should speak respectfully, pray, and seek mature pastoral counsel if they cannot reach agreement. If anyone is being harmed or threatened, safety comes first.

  > Scripture:
  > Acts 5:29: “Then Peter and the other apostles answered and said, We ought to obey God rather than men.”

  > Ephesians 5:25: “Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it;”

## MG-S02-HUS

  > Scripture does call wives to submit to their own husbands in the Lord (Ephesians 5:22), and it calls every believer to submit to one another in the fear of God (Ephesians 5:21).
